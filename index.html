02 / Approach

The method matters as much as the result.

A

Test the behavior

Use independent oracles, boundary cases, and failure cases to check whether an implementation meets its stated contract.

B

Respect the data

Keep synthetic examples labeled as synthetic. When observations share a subject, preserve that grouping during evaluation.

C

Make limits visible

Document what was measured, what was assumed, and what a small demonstration cannot establish.
